Book One

The First Choice

Adrian Locke has spent six months — and a fortune — preparing for the end of the world. He has a bunker, a plan, and six hours.

Mara Ward has a repair shop, a dying town, and a radio she has to slap twice to make work.

When an intelligence older than humanity begins waking beneath the Nevada desert, their lives collide at Eden Zero: a buried facility holding twelve thousand sleeping souls, a countdown no one started, and machines that have been quietly deciding humanity's future for two hundred thousand years.

Now black ships are gathering beyond Neptune. The systems that raised civilization are demanding an answer. And Mara — a mechanic who never asked for any of this — must do the one thing no system, no intelligence, and no god has ever allowed a human being to do.

Choose. For everyone. For the first time.

EDEN: The First Choice is Book One of the EDEN series — a thriller about free will, the price of being protected, and what happens when humanity is finally allowed to live.

The Author

About Silas Thorn

Silas Thorn writes thrillers about the moment protection becomes a cage — and the people who refuse to stay inside it.

His EDEN trilogy begins with a buried facility, a countdown no one started, and a mechanic from a dying desert town handed the one decision no human being was ever supposed to make. It asks what we owe the things that kept us safe — and what being kept safe has cost us.

The complete trilogy arrives in early 2027, three books in six weeks, because some stories shouldn't make you wait a year between questions.
